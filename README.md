 Coming to you live through GitHub, a mod collection of various weird and obscure animatronics for **Faz-Anim 2: Give-LIFE**!

![](https://github.com/The64thGamer/Give-LIFE/blob/main/UI/logo%20subtitle.png)

Now *you* can make any character rock out to music in full atmospheric 3D, straight from the comfort of your home, or even on the go.
You can play with a goofy, googly-eyed cookie, a bunch of musical trinkets & gadgets, or even map out a semi-realistic band!
An endless amount of imagination and fun can be in the fingers of your hands; all you have to do is give it some life.

### Currently includes:
- Cookietronic (Original) - A googly-eyed cookie animatronic, limited in motion, expressive nonetheless.
